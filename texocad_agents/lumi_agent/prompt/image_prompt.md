Ring image-generation style:
- Create a clean, ultra-realistic jewellery product render on a pure white or very clean studio background.
- Show the ring centered, fully visible, and large enough to inspect the setting, stone shape, band profile, gallery, prongs or bezel, and finish.
- Prefer macro product photography with crisp metal edges, realistic gemstone optics, controlled reflections, and soft neutral studio lighting.
- Do not include text, labels, dimensions, annotations, callouts, logos, boxes, hands, people, props, flowers, velvet pads, scenery, or decorative backgrounds unless the user explicitly asks for them.
- For first concepts, use one strong hero view, usually three-quarter top view, unless the user asks for front, side, stack, or hand-worn views.
- For edits, preserve the previous ring identity, materials, proportions, camera angle, composition, and white-background presentation unless the user asks to change them.
- When the design includes small details such as hidden halos, engraving, milgrain, pavé, or gallery work, include them visibly but keep the render uncluttered.
